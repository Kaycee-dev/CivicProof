## Non-claims

No corruption detection, accusation, theft/misappropriation conclusion, whole-life spending trace, verified treasury payment, present-day condition verification, national completeness, independent confirmation of source truth, legal certification, comprehensive WCAG audit or human usability study. Hashes do not prove truth. Neither identical contract numbers nor convenient amounts resolve C09/C12. `met` does not establish road completion. An absent search result does not establish project nonexistence.
